वरदविनायक

Varadvinayak

Mantra

ॐ वरदविनायकाय नमः
गणानां त्वा गणपतिं हवामहे कविं कवीनामुपमश्रवस्तमम् ।ज्येष्ठराजं ब्रह्मणां ब्रह्मणस्पत आ नः शृण्वन्नूतिभिः सीद सादनम् ॥

Rigveda 2.23.1. We call upon you, lord of the hosts, the sage of sages and most renowned, the eldest king of sacred utterances, Brahmanaspati. Come to us, listening, with your help, and take your seat.

About

Mahad lies near Khopoli and is the closest of the eight temples to Mumbai. Here Ganesha is worshipped as Varadvinayak, the giver of boons and success.

The idol was found in the adjoining lake in 1690 by Dhondu Paudkar, which explains its weathered look. The temple was built in 1725 by Ramji Mahadev Biwalkar. A tradition credits the sage Grutsamad, known for the Rigvedic verse Ganānām tvā, with founding it.

The idol faces east with its trunk to the left, beside an oil lamp said to have burned without a break since 1892. This is the only one of the eight where devotees may pay homage to the idol personally.
