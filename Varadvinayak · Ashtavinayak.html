Ashtavinayak · 4 of 8

वरदविनायक

Varadvinayak

Mahad, Raigad district

ॐ वरदविनायकाय नमः
गणानां त्वा गणपतिं हवामहे कविं कवीनामुपमश्रवस्तमम् ।ज्येष्ठराजं ब्रह्मणां ब्रह्मणस्पत आ नः शृण्वन्नूतिभिः सीद सादनम् ॥

Rigveda 2.23.1. We invoke you, Ganapati, lord of the hosts and wisest of sages. Come to us with your help and take your seat.

About

Mahad, near Khopoli, is the closest of the eight to Mumbai. The idol was found in a lake in 1690, and a lamp here is said to have burned without a break since 1892.

Name means: Giver of boons
