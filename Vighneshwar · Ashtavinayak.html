विघ्नेश्वर

Vighneshwar

Mantra

ॐ विघ्नेश्वराय नमः
विघ्नेश्वराय वरदाय सुरप्रियाय लम्बोदराय सकलाय जगद्धिताय ।नागाननाय श्रुतियज्ञविभूषिताय गौरीसुताय गणनाथ नमो नमस्ते ॥

Salutations to Ganesha: lord of obstacles and giver of boons, beloved of the gods, pot-bellied, complete, friend of the world, elephant-faced, adorned by the Vedas and sacrifices, son of Gauri.

About

Ozar stands on the Kukadi river. Legend says the demon Vighnasura was created by Indra to disrupt a sacrifice held by King Abhinandan, but he went on to destroy all Vedic rites. Ganesha defeated him, and the conquered demon asked that his own name be joined with Ganesha’s. The god is therefore known as Vighneshwar or Vighnahar.

The temple faces east and is enclosed by a high stone wall. Its pinnacle is made of gold, and it is thought to date to about 1785, possibly built by Chimaji Appa.

The idol faces east with its trunk to the left, rubies for eyes, a diamond on the forehead and a jewel in the navel. Riddhi and Siddhi are placed on either side.
