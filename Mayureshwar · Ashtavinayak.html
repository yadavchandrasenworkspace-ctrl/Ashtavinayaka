Ashtavinayak · Stop 1 of 8

मयूरेश्वर

Mayureshwar

Mantra

ॐ मयूरेश्वराय नमः
वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभ ।निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥

O Lord of the curved trunk and mighty body, whose splendour equals ten million suns, make all my undertakings free of obstacles, always.

About

Morgaon lies on the Karha river, about 55 km from Pune, and takes its name from the Marathi word for peacock. This is the first temple of the Ashtavinayak circuit, and the pilgrimage ends with a second visit here.

According to tradition, Ganesha rode a peacock and slew the demon Sindhu at this place, which is why he is worshipped as Mayureshwar.

The black-stone temple has four gates and four minarets. A Nandi, the bull of Shiva, sits before the entrance, which is unusual for a Ganesha temple. The idol has its trunk turned to the left, a cobra rising over it, and Siddhi and Riddhi beside it.
