Ashtavinayak · 1 of 8

मयूरेश्वर

Mayureshwar

Morgaon, Pune district

ॐ मयूरेश्वराय नमः
वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभ ।निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥

O Lord of the curved trunk and mighty body, shining like ten million suns, always remove obstacles from my work.

About

Morgaon, on the Karha river, is where the Ashtavinayak yatra begins and ends. Legend says Ganesha rode a peacock here and defeated the demon Sindhu. A Nandi bull stands before the temple, which is unusual for a Ganesha shrine.

Name means: Lord of the peacock
