विघ्नेश्वर

Vighneshwar

Ozar, near Junnar, Pune district

ॐ विघ्नेश्वराय नमः
विघ्नेश्वराय वरदाय सुरप्रियाय लम्बोदराय सकलाय जगद्धिताय ।नागाननाय श्रुतियज्ञविभूषिताय गौरीसुताय गणनाथ नमो नमस्ते ॥

Salutations to Ganesha, lord of obstacles and giver of boons, the elephant-faced son of Gauri, adorned by the Vedas and sacrifices.

About

Ozar stands on the Kukadi river. Legend says Ganesha defeated the demon Vighnasura, who was ruining sacred rituals, and was named Vighneshwar, the lord who removes obstacles. The temple’s spire is made of gold.

Name means: Lord of obstacles
