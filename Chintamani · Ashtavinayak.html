चिन्तामणि

Chintamani

Mantra

ॐ चिन्तामणये नमः
ॐ एकदन्ताय विद्महे ।वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ।तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् ॥

We know the one-tusked one and meditate on the curved-trunked one. May that tusked lord inspire us.

About

Theur lies about 22 km from Pune, where the Mula, Mutha and Bhima rivers meet. According to legend, Ganesha won back the precious Chintamani jewel from the greedy Guna for the sage Kapila, who then placed the jewel around Ganesha’s neck. That is how he became Chintamani Vinayak.

This happened under a kadamba tree, and Theur was once called Kadambanagar. The lake behind the temple is Kadambatirtha.

The idol faces east with its trunk to the left, with a carbuncle and diamonds for eyes. The outer wooden hall was built by the Peshwas. Chintamani is said to have been the family deity of Peshwa Madhavrao I, who, tradition says, died at the temple in 1772.
