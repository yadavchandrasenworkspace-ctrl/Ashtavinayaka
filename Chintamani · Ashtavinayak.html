Ashtavinayak · 5 of 8

चिन्तामणि

Chintamani

Theur, Pune district

ॐ चिन्तामणये नमः
ॐ एकदन्ताय विद्महे ।वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ।तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् ॥

We know the one-tusked one and meditate on the curved-trunked one. May he inspire us.

About

Theur lies where the Mula, Mutha and Bhima rivers meet. Legend says Ganesha won back the Chintamani jewel from the greedy Guna for the sage Kapila, and so became Chintamani Vinayak.

Name means: Lord of the Chintamani jewel
