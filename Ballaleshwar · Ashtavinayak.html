Ashtavinayak · Stop 3 of 8

बल्लाळेश्वर

Ballaleshwar

Mantra

ॐ बल्लाळेश्वराय नमः
शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥

Wearing white garments, all-pervading, moon-bright, four-armed, with a serene face: one should meditate on him to remove all obstacles.

About

Ballaleshwar is the only one of the eight known by the name of a devotee, a boy named Ballal.

According to the Ganesha Purana account, Ballal worshipped a stone image of Ganesha in the forest with his friends. His father, the merchant Kalyan, angered by the delays, threw the image away and tied the boy to a tree. Ganesha appeared, freed him, and at Ballal’s request made Pali his permanent home.

The idol faces east, has its trunk to the left, and has diamonds set in its eyes and navel. The temple was rebuilt in stone from an earlier wooden one by Nana Phadnavis in 1760. Behind it stands the shrine of Dhundi Vinayak, which faces west and is the image Kalyan threw away. The offering here is besan laddu rather than modak.
