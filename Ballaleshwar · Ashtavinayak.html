Ashtavinayak · 3 of 8

बल्लाळेश्वर

Ballaleshwar

Pali, Raigad district

ॐ बल्लाळेश्वराय नमः
शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥

Clad in white, all-pervading, moon-bright, four-armed and serene: meditate on him to remove all obstacles.

About

Pali is the only temple named after a devotee, a boy called Ballal. His angry father threw away the stone image Ballal worshipped and tied him to a tree. Ganesha appeared to free him and, at Ballal’s request, stayed in Pali.

Name means: Lord of Ballal
