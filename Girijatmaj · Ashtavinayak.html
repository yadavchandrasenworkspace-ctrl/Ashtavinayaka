Ashtavinayak · 6 of 8

गिरिजात्मज

Girijatmaj

Lenyadri, near Junnar, Pune district

ॐ गिरिजात्मजाय नमः
एकदन्तं महाकायं तप्तकाञ्चनसन्निभम् ।लम्बोदरं विशालाक्षं वन्देऽहं गणनायकम् ॥

I bow to the leader of the ganas: single-tusked, large-bodied, gleaming like molten gold, pot-bellied and wide-eyed.

About

Girijatmaj means son of Girija, another name of Parvati, who is said to have prayed here for a son. The temple is cut into a hill of Buddhist caves at Lenyadri, near Junnar, and is reached by about 300 steps.

Name means: Son of Girija (Parvati)
