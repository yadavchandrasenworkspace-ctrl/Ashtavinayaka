गिरिजात्मज

Girijatmaj

Mantra

ॐ गिरिजात्मजाय नमः
एकदन्तं महाकायं तप्तकाञ्चनसन्निभम् ।लम्बोदरं विशालाक्षं वन्देऽहं गणनायकम् ॥

I bow to the leader of the ganas: single-tusked, large-bodied, gleaming like molten gold, pot-bellied and wide-eyed.

About

Girijatmaj means the son of Girija, a name of Parvati. Tradition says Parvati performed penance at this spot to be blessed with Ganesha as her son.

The temple is cut into a hill of Buddhist caves known as Ganesh Lena, and is reached by a climb of about 300 steps. Its cave hall is wide and has no supporting pillars, and daylight lights it throughout the day.

The idol faces north with its trunk to the left, and it is worshipped from behind.
