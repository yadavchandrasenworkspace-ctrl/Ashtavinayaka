महागणपति

Mahaganapati

Mantra

ॐ महागणपतये नमः
ॐ नमस्ते गणपतये ।त्वमेव प्रत्यक्षं तत्त्वमसि ।त्वमेव केवलं कर्ताऽसि ॥

Salutations to you, Ganapati. You alone are the visible truth. You alone are the creator.

About

Ranjangaon lies about 50 km from Pune, and this is the last temple of the circuit before the pilgrim returns to Morgaon. According to tradition, Shiva worshipped Ganesha here before fighting the demon Tripurasura, and the town was then called Manipur.

The temple faces east and is built so that, during the sun’s southward movement, its rays fall directly on the idol. The seated Mahaganapati has his trunk to the left, with Riddhi and Siddhi on either side. Peshwa Madhavrao built the stone sanctum around the idol.

A popular belief holds that the original idol, called Mahotkat, with ten trunks and twenty arms, is hidden in a vault below. The temple authorities deny that any such idol exists.
