Ashtavinayak · 8 of 8

महागणपति

Mahaganapati

Ranjangaon, Pune district

ॐ महागणपतये नमः
ॐ नमस्ते गणपतये ।त्वमेव प्रत्यक्षं तत्त्वमसि ।त्वमेव केवलं कर्ताऽसि ॥

Salutations to you, Ganapati. You alone are the visible truth. You alone are the creator.

About

Ranjangaon is the last temple on the yatra before pilgrims return to Morgaon. Tradition says Shiva worshipped Ganesha here before fighting the demon Tripurasura. The idol sits with Riddhi and Siddhi on either side.

Name means: The great lord of the ganas
