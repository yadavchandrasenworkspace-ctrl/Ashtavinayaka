सिद्धिविनायक

Siddhivinayak

Siddhatek, Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar) district

ॐ सिद्धिविनायकाय नमः
गजाननं भूतगणादिसेवितं कपित्थजम्बूफलचारुभक्षणम् ।उमासुतं शोकविनाशकारकं नमामि विघ्नेश्वरपादपङ्कजम् ॥

I bow to the feet of Vighneshwara, the elephant-faced son of Uma, who ends sorrow and is served by hosts of spirits.

About

Siddhatek stands on the Bhima river. Legend says Vishnu prayed to Ganesha here before defeating the demons Madhu and Kaitabha. It is the only one of the eight with its trunk turned to the right, and pilgrims walk around the hill the temple stands on.

Name means: Giver of success
