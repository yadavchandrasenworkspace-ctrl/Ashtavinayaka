सिद्धिविनायक

Siddhivinayak

Mantra

ॐ सिद्धिविनायकाय नमः
गजाननं भूतगणादिसेवितं कपित्थजम्बूफलचारुभक्षणम् ।उमासुतं शोकविनाशकारकं नमामि विघ्नेश्वरपादपङ्कजम् ॥

I bow to the feet of Vighneshwara, son of Uma and destroyer of sorrow: the elephant-faced lord, served by hosts of spirits, who delights in the wood-apple and jambu fruit.

About

Siddhatek stands on the bank of the Bhima river. According to regional legend, Vishnu worshipped Ganesha here before he defeated the demons Madhu and Kaitabha.

This is the only idol of the eight whose trunk turns to the right. It is about three feet tall, faces north, and has Siddhi and Riddhi seated on one thigh. The inner sanctum was built by Ahilyabai Holkar.

The temple stands on a small hillock, and devotees make their circumambulation (pradakshina) by walking around the hillock.
